Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Fix Human Sleep: Funniest Patch Note"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-yuki-kato-fix-human-sleep-funniest-patch-note-f6ad4c27): one line of patch notes announcing that human sleep has been fixed — funny enough to make a reader laugh, plausible enough to sit in a real changelog.

The joke treats falling asleep like shipping software: the old version made you sit through a 45-minute loading screen of every embarrassing thing you have ever said before it would let you rest.
The fix is specific and unexpected rather than a flat claim that sleep now works, and it trusts the reader to get the joke without explaining it.
